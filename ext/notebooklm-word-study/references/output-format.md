# Output Format

## Markdown Word List Template

```markdown
# Word Study Report
**Notebook**: {notebook_name} | **Date**: {date} | **Language**: {language}

---

## {word} — {topic}

**Definition**: {definition derived from sources}

### Examples

1. "{example sentence}" — *{source_title}*
2. "{example sentence}" — *{source_title}*
3. "{example sentence}" — *{source_title}*

### Related Forms
- {collocations, word variations, synonyms found in sources}

---
```

## Example Output

```markdown
# Word Study Report
**Notebook**: Word Study: resilience | **Date**: 2026-03-07 | **Language**: English

---

## resilience — psychology

**Definition**: The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties and adapt positively to adversity, trauma, or stress.

### Examples

1. "Resilience is not a trait that people either have or do not have." — *APA.org*
2. "Children who develop resilience are better able to face disappointment." — *Harvard Health Blog*
3. "Resilience training has been shown to reduce burnout in healthcare workers." — *Journal of Occupational Health*

### Related Forms
- resilient (adj), resiliently (adv), build/develop/foster resilience

---

## resilience — ecology

**Definition**: The ability of an ecosystem to absorb disturbance and reorganize while undergoing change.

### Examples

1. "The resilience of coral reefs is threatened by rising ocean temperatures." — *Nature.com*
2. "Forest resilience depends on biodiversity and structural complexity." — *Ecology Letters*

### Related Forms
- ecological resilience, community resilience, adaptive capacity

---
```

## File Naming

Default output path: `./word-study-{YYYYMMDD}.md`

Append new word sections to existing file if studying multiple words across sessions.
